Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Orbit is an app that helps you keep in touch with the people in your network. This policy explains what information Orbit handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have. In short: your network is stored on your phone, it is copied to the cloud only if you create an account, and we never sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it to track you.

Summary
  • Without an account, everything stays on your phone.
  • With an account, your network is backed up so you can restore it on a new phone.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no selling or sharing of your data.
  • You can delete any contact, or your whole account, at any time from the app.

1. Information stored on your phone

Everything you add to Orbit is saved in a database on your device. This includes the people you add and the details you choose to record about them (such as name, profession, company, phone number, email address, birthday, partner and children's names, ages and birthdays, interests, how you met and notes), the reach-out history you log, your reminder schedules and your app settings. This information does not leave your phone unless you create an account (section 3).

2. Contacts permission

If you choose Import from my contacts, Orbit asks for permission to read your phone's address book so it can show you a list to pick from. Only the people you select are saved in Orbit, along with their name, job title, company, first phone number, first email address, birthday, notes and photo thumbnail from your address book. Orbit does not upload your address book, and it does not read it at any other time.

3. Optional account and cloud backup

You can create an account with an email address and password to back up your network and use it on more than one phone. If you do:

  • Account information: your email address and a password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see them.
  • Backed-up data: the information described in section 1 is copied to our cloud database and kept in sync across your devices.
  • Technical information: our hosting provider records standard server logs (such as IP address, time and request details) for security and reliability. These are kept only for a short period.

Your backed-up data is protected so that only your signed-in account can read or change it. It is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

4. Notifications

Reminders and birthday notifications are scheduled and delivered by your phone itself. Orbit does not use a push-notification server, and no notification content is sent anywhere.

5. How we use information

We use your information only to provide the app's features: showing your network, scheduling your reminders, backing up and syncing your data, and sending account emails (confirming your email address, resetting your password, and letting you know when your password changes). We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

We use a small number of providers to run the optional account features. They process data only on our behalf and only to provide their service:

We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else, except if required by law.

7. Information about other people

The details you record in Orbit are usually about other people. That information is stored only for you, is visible only to you, and is never used to contact those people or for any other purpose. Please record only information you are comfortable keeping, and remove it if someone asks you to.

8. How long we keep information

  • On your phone: until you delete it in the app, sign out, or uninstall the app.
  • In the cloud: for as long as your account exists. When you delete a contact, its details are erased from the cloud and only an empty placeholder remains so your other devices know to remove it.
  • When you delete your account: your account and all backed-up data are deleted immediately. Copies may remain in our provider's encrypted backups for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.

9. Your choices and rights

  • View, edit and delete any contact or note in the app at any time.
  • Use Orbit without an account, so nothing is stored in the cloud.
  • Sign out to remove your network from a phone while keeping your backup.
  • Delete your account and backup from the app. See how to delete your account.
  • Contact us to request a copy of your data, a correction or deletion, or to ask any question about your privacy. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honour them.

10. Children

Orbit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit and at rest and access rules that limit each account to its own data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you as required by law if a breach affects it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for significant changes we will let you know in the app.

13. Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@orbitnetwork.app
