Keep your network in orbit

Orbit reminds you to reach out to the people who matter, on a schedule you choose, with notes on what to talk about. Private by default: your network stays on your phone unless you turn on backup.

Reminders on your schedule

Set how often to check in with each person, from weekly to once a year, and get a nudge at the right time.

Remember the little things

Save birthdays, kids' names and ages, interests and notes, so every conversation picks up where you left off.

Yours alone

No ads, no tracking. Optional backup lets you restore your network on a new phone.
